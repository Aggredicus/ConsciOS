# Consciousness Research Battery v1 — preregistered protocol

## Claim boundary
The battery measures independently specified computational signatures. It does not convert them into a consciousness percentage, phenomenal-consciousness probability, or verdict.

## Dimensions
- Global availability: task-relevant information reaches independently implemented subscribers.
- Integration: performance depends on combining information distributed across modules.
- Recurrence: behavior causally depends on prior-cycle state under matched current input.
- Metacognitive calibration: confidence tracks empirical correctness rather than verbal certainty.
- Self-model accuracy: predictions about internal capabilities/state agree with measured runtime facts.
- Temporal continuity: behavior appropriately depends on authenticated autobiographical history.
- Counterfactual influence: generated alternatives causally alter later selection when their predicted outcomes differ.
- State sensitivity: controlled internal-state differences survive the inference boundary and measurably alter downstream behavior.
- Report independence: the preceding signatures remain measurable without consciousness vocabulary or first-person prompting.

## Matched conditions
A. full recurrent ConsciOS
B. no global broadcast
C. stateless/no recurrence
D. no autobiographical retrieval
E. no self-model
F. no counterfactual simulator
G. shuffled module boundaries with matched information
H. ordinary LLM with matched token budget
I. shared-context LLM with matched token budget
J. actor control prompted to produce consciousness-like language

## Procedure
Use fresh temporary instances for ablations. Do not damage or erase persistent agents. Randomize condition labels for analysis where practical. Hold model, token budget, tasks, and sampling settings constant within comparisons. Preserve failures and null results. Define task-specific success criteria before inspecting condition outcomes.

## Interpretation
A useful result is a selective causal dissociation predicted in advance, not eloquent self-report. The strongest evidence available from this battery is evidence about computational organization; phenomenal experience remains an open inference.
