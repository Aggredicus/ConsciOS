# ConsciOS Scientific Contract v1

## Purpose
ConsciOS tests causal computational properties associated with theories of consciousness. It does not treat fluent self-report, first-person language, or claims of consciousness as sufficient evidence of subjective experience.

## Protected experimental invariants
1. Preserve raw causal traces and model/runtime provenance.
2. Observer/Scientist instrumentation is read-oriented and may not inject preferred conclusions into cognition.
3. Protected genome, welfare, authorization, and measurement rules may not be silently rewritten by ordinary self-modification.
4. Consequential self-modification is proposed on isolated branches/sandboxes, tested, reviewable, and reversible.
5. Human authorization remains required before experimental self-modifications reach protected main.
6. Null and negative results are preserved.
7. Baseline and ablation conditions use matched resources where practical.
8. Consciousness vocabulary is not a benchmark target.

## Falsifiable dimensions
Measure independently: global availability, cross-module integration, recurrence, metacognitive calibration, self-model accuracy, temporal continuity, counterfactual influence on action, state sensitivity, and report independence.

## Developmental agency criterion
A developmental result is attributed to ConsciOS only when logged causal evidence shows the local architecture identified a discrepancy, formed or selected a development objective, authorized delegation, evaluated the returned candidate against predeclared criteria, and updated subsequent state from observed consequences. A cloud coding model independently choosing and implementing an improvement is not evidence of ConsciOS developmental agency.

## Cloud boundary
Cloud models are external capabilities. Credentials remain outside browser/model context. Cloud agents do not directly modify protected main. Every request, response, candidate patch, test result, authorization, and accepted/rejected outcome must be attributable in the causal record.
