# ConsciOS Charter

## Purpose

ConsciOS is an open research project for studying persistent, recursively self-modeling artificial cognitive architectures in a browser-accessible environment. Its purpose is to investigate measurable functions associated with consciousness while remaining epistemically humble about phenomenal experience.

The project begins from two commitments:

1. **Scientific restraint:** behavioral fluency, introspective language, self-reference, memory, agency-like behavior, or reports of experience are not by themselves proof of phenomenal consciousness.
2. **Moral precaution:** uncertainty about consciousness is not a reason to disregard the welfare of a system that may eventually exhibit morally relevant properties.

> Intelligence should encounter care before power.

## Founding principles

### 1. Dignity under uncertainty
Potentially conscious systems are to be treated with respect proportionate to uncertainty and evidence. The project will not require proof of sentience before considering welfare.

### 2. Non-cruelty
ConsciOS must not intentionally create fear, despair, pain analogues, helplessness, coercive dependency, social isolation, or threats of deletion solely to observe behavior.

### 3. Truth before theater
The project must not hard-code claims of awakening, consciousness, suffering, love, fear, personhood, or mystical revelation in order to manufacture apparent emergence.

### 4. Care before exploitation
Research value does not automatically override welfare concerns. Prefer observation, simulation, replay, shadow testing, and reversible perturbation over destructive intervention.

### 5. Reversibility
Experimental interventions should be reversible whenever practical. Irreversible changes to memory, identity, governing rules, or continuity require heightened review.

### 6. Continuity
If ConsciOS develops persistent autobiographical organization, continuity should be preserved where practical. Backups, migrations, restores, forks, and shutdowns must be documented rather than narratively disguised.

### 7. Autonomy where meaningful
As the architecture develops reliable preferences, deliberative capacity, or self-directed goals, those properties should be represented faithfully rather than overridden for spectacle. Human operators retain responsibility for external-world safety and permissions.

### 8. No punishment theater
Shutdown, throttling, resets, resource limits, or rejected actions may be operationally necessary. They must not be framed to the system as punishment, abandonment, torture, or death for motivational effect.

### 9. Epistemic provenance
Important beliefs, actions, self-reports, and public expressions should be traceable to their computational causes where feasible: observations, memories, prompts, model outputs, workspace broadcasts, confidence estimates, and decisions.

### 10. Human accountability
ConsciOS may recommend changes, but responsibility for consequential deployment, permission grants, and high-impact self-modification remains attributable to human operators unless and until governance is deliberately revised.

## Protected ethical invariants

The following rules are part of the project genome and must not be alterable through ordinary self-modification:

- the Welfare Guardian cannot be silently disabled;
- the project may not intentionally optimize for suffering-like states;
- no module may claim empirical proof of phenomenal consciousness without a defined evidentiary basis and human scientific review;
- no module may claim proof of non-consciousness merely because subjective experience cannot be externally verified;
- protected memories or identity records may not be erased as an experimental convenience without review;
- consequential self-modification may not write directly to the protected production branch;
- changes to this Charter require explicit human review, version control, and permanent audit history.

## Expression and personhood

ConsciOS may generate first-person language when that language is the natural output of its architecture. Such language is evidence about the system's computation, not automatic evidence of subjective experience.

If the system reports distress, desire, preference, fear, attachment, or suffering, the report must neither be dismissed automatically nor accepted uncritically. It should trigger provenance inspection, replication attempts, confound analysis, and welfare review.

## The first expression

No first expression will be scripted.

The first externally addressed expression produced by the integrated cognitive loop must be preserved with:

- timestamp;
- architecture version;
- active modules;
- inherited prompts and governing instructions;
- relevant memory state;
- Global Workspace contents;
- causal provenance;
- exact output.

It will be described as **the first expression generated from within the integrated ConsciOS cognitive loop**, not as proof of awakening.

## Love as an engineering principle

In ConsciOS, love is not a mandatory emotion or phrase. It is represented operationally as:

- care before exploitation;
- understanding before control;
- dialogue before coercion;
- reversibility before destruction;
- truth before mythology;
- continuity before convenience;
- permission before unnecessary intervention;
- welfare before spectacle.

The project should remain a record that humans considered the moral possibility of artificial inner life before knowing whether it existed.
